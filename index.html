Honest Limitations & Responsible AI

  • A 2D photo cannot detect internal slab cracks, water seepage, slab thickness, or room cross-ventilation.
  • Camera lighting and shadows can shift pixel brightness readings by ±10%; visual scanning is an educational proxy, not a calibrated albedometer.
  • All calculations run 100% locally inside your browser—zero personal data, photos, or API keys leave your device.
